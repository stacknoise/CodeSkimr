## Budgets

Measured with `tools/acceptance/m4-measure.mjs`: it starts the end-to-end server of the app (release build), scans a folder (CodeSkimr itself: 703 files, 11,800 symbols, 145,479 references, 72,129 lines, first scan 1.4 s) and calls the REST commands with timing in the client, so every number includes the HTTP round trip on the loopback address. The raw output of the run is in `docs/acceptance/m4/measure-output.txt`. The project is larger than in the earlier runs (594 files and 94,949 references in the second), so the figures are not comparable one to one. The first scan takes 1.4 s for 72k lines (the CI benchmark `scan/full_100k_loc` reports about 0.7 s for 100k lines); on this container that is over the budget of 1 s per 100k lines when extrapolated, the Budgets thread owns it.

### Usages query at depth 2 (`usages.query`)

| Incoming usages of the symbol | Symbols | Median (first call) | p95 | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 9 | 137 | 3.2 ms | 6.7 ms | 8.0 ms |
| 10 to 99 | 43 | 4.2 ms | 6.2 ms | 6.5 ms |
| 100 to 999 | 168 | 7.3 ms | 9.4 ms | 194.8 ms (single call) |
| 1,000 and more | 52 | 8.0 ms | 10.3 ms | 10.6 ms |

The 400 symbols come from ten searches (`new`, `error`, `path` and so on); each was queried once with `way = both`, `depth = 2`, tests included. The largest symbols have 13,808, 3,279 and 2,004 incoming usages. The five symbols with the most usages were then queried 50 times each: median 9.6 ms over all 250 calls, p95 11.8 ms, max 14.1 ms. Largest symbol: `both` at depth 1: 9.3 ms, `incoming` at depth 1: 9.6 ms, `incoming` at depth 2: 10.4 ms, `outgoing` at depth 2: 5.2 ms (medians; p95 up to 13.1 ms). The one outlier of 195 ms (bucket 100 to 999) is a single first call. A similar one (193 ms) was seen in the first run, so it is not a one-off; the budget is the median and p95 of repeated calls, which are met, but the cause of the cold call is open (#333). The status bar showed "Abfrage 220 ms" after opening the usages screen in the screenshot run, probably the same effect. The raw output of this run is in `docs/acceptance/m4/measure-output.txt`.

## Comparison with the mocks

The mock sources in `docs/mocks/` are written for a design canvas whose runtime is not in the repository, and the published canvas is private. `tools/acceptance/render-mock.mjs` fills the small template language of a mock (`{{name}}`, `<sc-for>`) from the mock's own data function and renders it with Chromium at 1440 × 900. `tools/acceptance/m4-screens.mjs` takes the screenshots of the real UI over the end-to-end server in German for each of the three themes Hell, Petrol and Dunkel. The mocks of screens 3, 5 and 6 are drawn in Hell (blueprint 15.1), so their colours follow the active theme (15.3). The mocks hold sample data, so only structure, proportions, labels and behaviour are compared, not the content. The comparison is by eye plus the inline values of the mocks; a pixel diff would be meaningless with different data. The `mock-*.png` files are from the first run (the mocks did not change); the `real-<screen>-<theme>.png` files are new in this run.

Images are in `docs/acceptance/m4/`: `mock-*.png` is the mock, `real-*.png` the application.

## Verdict

M4 can be accepted with named leftovers. Criteria 2, 3 and 4 are met: the graph layout (about 1 ms against 50 ms), the usages query (median 9.6 ms, p95 11.8 ms, max 14.1 ms against 20 ms for the symbols with the most usages, apart from one first call of 195 ms, #333) and the jump from the explorer, now also with `Alt` + click. Criterion 1 is partly met: the frame, the layer bands, the namespace level with depth, the type focus, the scan screen and the usages screen follow the mocks in structure and behaviour in all three themes. What is left is small and named above: canvas for very many nodes and the C# layers (#198, #145), the legend and the wrapped name on the usages screen (#335). Whether the screens are close enough to the mocks is a judgement by eye for the product owner; the screenshots are in `docs/acceptance/m4/`.
